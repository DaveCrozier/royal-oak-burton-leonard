      Walk here. Earn the pint.

      Burton Leonard sits on the Ripon Rowel Walk, with farmland, canal towpath and quiet lanes right on the doorstep. Dogs are welcome in our beer gardens.

      On foot

      On two wheels

      More rides: quiet lanes link Burton Leonard, Bishop Monkton and Copgrove. Komoot's cycling guide around Bishop Monkton is a good place to start planning a loop.

      Further afield: Fountains Abbey, Newby Hall, Hackfall Woods and Markenfield Hall are all on or near the Rowel.

      Walk details from Yorkshire.com and Walking Britain; ride maps from Komoot. Check the route and conditions before you set out.
